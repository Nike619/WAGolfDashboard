Ground conditions
Drainage scores (1–5) come from golfer reviews and course-construction write-ups. Where golfers haven't written about a course, the score comes from the USDA soil survey of the native ground (sand and gravel drain fast, clay and floodplains slowly); it can't see sand caps or drain tile added when the course was built. Ground condition combines the score with rain over the three days before and on the day itself. It is an estimate; call ahead after a big storm.
Courses

To add, remove or pause a course, edit data/courses.json in the GitHub repo (open it on GitHub ↗). A course needs a name, access ("public" or "private"), lat/lon, and for tee times a platform with its params. Set "active": false to pause one. Tee times and rain update every morning at about 6:50 AM Pacific; "Check now" starts one.
