Private clubs

Members and guests · call the pro shop

Private clubs don't publish tee times. Use this to see which club is playing driest before you ask your member friend or set up reciprocal play. Tap a club for its drainage notes and rain.

Drainage scores (1–5) come from golfer reviews and course-construction write-ups; many private and small-town clubs have little public discussion and default to 3 with low confidence. Ground condition combines the score with rain over the three days before and on the day itself. It is an estimate; call ahead after a big storm.

Manage courses

To add, remove or pause a course, edit data/courses.json in the GitHub repo (open it on GitHub ↗). A course needs a name, access ("public" or "private"), lat/lon, and for tee times a platform with its params. Set "active": false to pause one. Tee times and rain update every morning at about 6:50 AM Pacific; "Run a check" starts one now.
